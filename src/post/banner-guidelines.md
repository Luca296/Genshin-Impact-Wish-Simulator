## Custom banner artwork

Choose local artwork for the splash and face images. A transparent PNG or WebP works well, and a close-up is useful for the face image. Fill in the banner fields and select its featured items before saving.

Custom banners are stored in the desktop profile. The editor exports a local backup rather than publishing a banner online. Export a backup if you want to move your banners or protect them against data loss. Importing a backup replaces the destination save rather than merging inventories. Remote image URLs and cloud-only artwork are not available offline.
